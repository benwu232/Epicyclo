Privacy Policy

At Epicyclo, we are fiercely committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle information in connection with our macOS application and website.

1. Zero Data Collection Architecture

Epicyclo does not collect, track, transmit, or sell any personal data, keystrokes, gestures, or browsing activity. The application functions entirely locally on your Mac using native driver hardware events.

2. Zero Accessibility Permissions

Epicyclo is deliberately engineered without requiring Accessibility (AX) permissions. We never intercept, observe, or inspect your keystrokes or onscreen window contents.

3. Payments & Licensing

License purchases for Epicyclo Premium are processed directly through Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record). We do not store your credit card or billing credentials.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy, please contact us at epicyclo232@gmail.com.
